As of May 4, 2026, a number of networks with a `provider_name` have a lower
`anonymizer_confidence` of 30.

Previously, all networks with `provider_name` had an `anonymizer_confidence`
of 99.

This change will be reflected in the following products and services:

- GeoIP Anonymous Plus database
- GeoIP Insights web service
