Effective January 20, 2020, we will be flagging more Cable/DSL networks used by
businesses (i.e. IP networks from ISPs that serve mid-small sized businesses) as
`Corporate` in the
[GeoIP2 Connection Type database](https://www.maxmind.com/en/geoip-databases).
Previously these types of networks were more often flagged as `Cable/DSL`.
