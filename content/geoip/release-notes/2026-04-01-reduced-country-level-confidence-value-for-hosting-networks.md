As part of our ongoing efforts to improve our geolocation confidence factors, we
have reduced the country-level confidence for some hosting networks in
circumstances where we are less confident about where the data centers
identified are deployed.

This change should not impact the confidence for residential, cellular, and
other networks commonly used by end-users.
