We have released new inputs for all minFraud web service customers. They
describe the most recent verification of the email address.

| new input                           | description                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `email/was_verification_successful` | Whether the most recent verification of the email address succeeded. [Learn more on our developer portal.](/minfraud/api-documentation/requests/#schema--request--email__was_verification_successful) |
| `email/verification_time`           | The date and time of the most recent verification of the email address. [Learn more on our developer portal.](/minfraud/api-documentation/requests/#schema--request--email__verification_time)        |
