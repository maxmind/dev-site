We will be changing the general time of day when we build our GeoIP Anonymous IP
and GeoIP Anonymous Plus datasets.

The build will now take place around 07:00 UTC instead of the previous time of
15:15 UTC. Please note that these times are not exact.

As a reminder, database builds can be delayed or released at any time. While we
will generally build these databases at the times detailed above, we recommend
setting up your integration to check for new databases periodically, and
recommend downloading and integrating whenever there's a new database available.
[See details about database update recommendations.](/geoip/updating-databases/)
