Our [GeoLite2 ASN database](/geoip/docs/databases) is now provided in a CSV
format suitable for importing into a SQL database as well as a GeoLite2 ASN
database in our MaxMind DB binary format. You can
[download the database here](/geoip/geolite2-free-geolocation-data/). GeoLite2
databases may also be downloaded and updated with our
[GeoIP Update program](/geoip/updating-databases/).
