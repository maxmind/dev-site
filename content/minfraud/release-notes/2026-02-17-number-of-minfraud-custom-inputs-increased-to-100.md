You are now able to add up to 100 custom inputs in minFraud.

Custom inputs allow you to pass data that is relevant to your business and
fine-tune minFraud custom rules to help identify suspicious transactions and
reduce fraud.

Custom inputs are available to users of all minFraud service tiers: Score,
Insights, and Factors.

[Learn how to use custom inputs on our Knowledge Base.](https://support.maxmind.com/knowledge-base/articles/use-custom-inputs-minfraud)
