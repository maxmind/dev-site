Our IP geolocation databases released on Tuesday, March 4, 2025 will be
significantly larger than our last release.

This increase in size is expected and affects the following databases:

- GeoIP City database
- GeoIP Country database
- GeoIP Enterprise database
- GeoLite City database
- GeoLite Country database
