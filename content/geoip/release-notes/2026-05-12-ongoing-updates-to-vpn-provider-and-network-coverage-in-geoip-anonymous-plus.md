We are monitoring changes in our coverage of VPN providers and networks in our
GeoIP Anonymous Plus database. A small reduction in total IP addresses and
providers is expected as we fine-tune and remove lower confidence networks from
our dataset.

You can expect to see fluctuations in the number of networks flagged with a VPN
confidence, last seen date, and confidence score. These changes are ongoing
improvements to the accuracy of our feed.

If these changes present challenges, please reach out to your Customer Success
Manager.

These changes will be reflected in the following products and services:

- GeoIP Anonymous Plus database
- GeoIP Insights web service
