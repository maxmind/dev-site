We have released a free IP geolocation API, which allows users to query our
GeoLite2 data. See our blog for
[an overview of the GeoLite2 web service](https://blog.maxmind.com/geolite2-web-service-free-ip-geolocation-api/).
You can learn how to integrate the GeoLite2 web service in our
[Getting Started with GeoLite2 guide](https://support.maxmind.com/knowledge-base/articles/test-the-web-services).
