We will soon be distinguishing AT&T Internet Air connections from the broader
pool of AT&T Wireless networks.

Some networks previously labeled `AT&T Wireless` will have their ISP name
updated to `AT&T Internet Air`.

This change will go live on Tuesday, August 26, 2025 and will be reflected in
the following products and services:

- GeoIP ISP database
- GeoIP Enterprise database
- GeoIP City Plus web service
- GeoIP Insights web service
